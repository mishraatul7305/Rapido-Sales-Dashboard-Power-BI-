# Rapido-Sales-Dashboard-Power-BI-
An interactive Power BI dashboard analysing sales, orders, pricing and payment behaviour for a delivery/vehicle business.
## Key Metrics
- Total Sales: 8.41M
- Total Quantity: 2K units
- Records analysed: 600
- Median unit price: 1.23K | Std. deviation of unit price: 12.98K

## Visuals Used
- Gauge and donut charts (sales, unit price by quantity)
- KPI cards (std. deviation, median, count, quantity)
- Area/line chart (order count and sales trends)
- Funnel charts (payment methods, profit by quantity)

## Insights
- Cash on Delivery is the most used payment method (142 orders),
  followed by Net Banking (125) and Debit Card (115).
- Order volume peaks at quantity 3 (182 orders) and drops sharply after 3.
- Quantity 3 orders generate the highest profit (540.60K).
